# Common seed scripts

Locale-agnostic seed SQL referenced from `seeds/seed.manifest.json` profiles.

- `001_bootstrap.sql` — deterministic standard-profile marker.
- `002_agent_catalog.sql` — official 58-agent catalog neutral base: ids, codes,
  taxonomy, version snapshots, composition slots, and capability references.
  Display names, descriptions, system prompts, and skill instructions are
  intentionally unset here and supplied by the locale seeds.
- `003_expert_catalog.sql` — expert catalog neutral base: the 22 expert
  categories, 331 expert agents and their initial versions, 5 expert teams and
  15 ordered team members. Display content, prompts and persona bodies are
  supplied by the locale seeds; the catalog is authored in
  `sdkwork-specs/specs/expert-catalog`.

`002_agent_catalog.sql` is generated by `scripts/generate-agent-catalog-seeds.mjs`;
`003_expert_catalog.sql` is generated by `scripts/generate-expert-seeds.mjs`.
Do not hand-edit either file.
